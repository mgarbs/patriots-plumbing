What “Master Plumber–led” actually means

Plumbing licenses come in tiers, and the difference matters more than most homeowners are ever told. A Master Plumber is the level that can design a system, pull the permit, and sign off on the work — not simply perform it under somebody else’s license from an office three counties away.

In practice that means the person setting the standard on your job is on your job. When a repair turns out to be a symptom of something larger — a slab leak behind a dripping fixture, a vent problem behind a slow drain — the diagnosis happens on site, not on a phone call to a supervisor who has never seen it.

It also means we will tell you when the answer is cheaper than you expected. If a part will genuinely buy you a few more years, we say so rather than selling you the replacement.

Two names, one shop

You may see us under two names, and both are us. The business is licensed as The Patriot’s Plumber, LLC — that is the name on the license, on the Google listing, and on anything official. Patriot’s Plumbing is the name we trade under, and the one you will see on invoices and on the truck.

If a document says one and the sign says the other, nothing is wrong. Same license, same plumber, same guarantee.

Residential and commercial

Residential work is the bulk of what we do — repairs, water heaters, drains, and the kitchen, bathroom and laundry remodels our office takes on. On the commercial side we handle repairs and installations for businesses, rental property and buildings across the same service area.

There is also work we deliberately do not take on, and we would rather say so than waste your visit. If you are unsure whether your project is a fit, describe it on the phone — you will get a straight answer either way.
